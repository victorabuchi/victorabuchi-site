Discovery

Every project starts with a conversation. A quick call or a written brief lets me understand the problem, the people it affects, and what success looks like before anything is designed.

Start a project See the full process
  • A clear problem statement

What happens

In this step.

Understand the problem

What is slow, manual or broken today, and what the current process really looks like from the inside.

Meet the people who use it

The people who live with the problem know it best. On Rannikon the hour formulas changed several times as workers and payroll staff weighed in.

Note the real-world limits

Devices, connectivity and languages. Rannikon’s workers use cheap Android phones, in fields with poor signal, in languages other than English.

Define what success looks like

A shared, plain-language picture of the outcome, so everyone agrees when it is done.
