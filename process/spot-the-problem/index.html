Spot the problem

Some projects start with me noticing something slow, manual or error-prone that people live with every day, and deciding it is worth fixing.

Start a project See the full process

What happens

In this step.

Notice the friction

Paper forms, notebooks, messages passed along by hand: the places where good people lose hours.

Look at the whole flow

Follow the work from the first person who touches it to the last.

Ask who feels it

Workers, supervisors, managers and payroll each experience the same problem differently.

Decide it is worth solving

A problem worth building for is one people depend on every day.
