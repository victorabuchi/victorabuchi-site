About

Second-year Bachelor's student at the University of Eastern Finland. I develop web services, Android and iOS mobile apps, Shopify apps, Shopify themes, and custom websites.

Role

Student Ambassador, Department of Computer Science, Faculty of Natural Sciences, Forestry and Technology, University of Eastern Finland. victor.abuchi@uef.fi

Building

Two SaaS products designed and built from scratch: Onshipy, dropshipping automation for resellers that imports from Nike, ASOS, Amazon and 900+ brands and sells through Shopify, and Swifrly, a financial layer connecting crypto to the real world: trading, payments, and card spending in one app.

Freelance

Most recently, a full-stack workforce management platform for Rannikon Puutarha, a berry farm in Finland: now running for 300+ seasonal workers across 6 house groups in 5 languages. Read the case study.

Why

I like building things people actually depend on: real workers, real deadlines, real problems that don't disappear if the code is sloppy. Software has to work, not just demo well.
