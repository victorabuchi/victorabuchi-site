How I work

Two ways to start.

Some projects begin with your brief. Others begin with a problem I noticed and decided to fix.

More on the process

You bring the brief

Your brief, my build.

For client work: we agree the scope first, then I build to it.

  1. 1

    Discovery

    A quick call or written brief to understand the problem, the audience, and what success looks like.

  2. 2

    Proposal

    A clear scope, timeline, and price, agreed on before any code gets written.

  3. 3

    Build

    Custom-coded, with regular check-ins so there are no surprises at handoff.

  4. 4

    Launch

    Deployed, tested, and handed off with everything documented.

  5. 5

    Support

    Ongoing fixes and features from someone who already knows the codebase.

I bring the solution

Spot it, solve it, present it.

For problems I notice first: I do the work up front, then show it to the people it’s for.

  1. 1

    Spot the problem

    Something slow, manual, or error-prone that people live with every day.

  2. 2

    Identify what’s broken

    Work out the real cause, and who it affects, before writing any code.

  3. 3

    Solve it

    Build the complete solution on my own, from scratch, until it works.

  4. 4

    Present it

    Show it to the company or organization involved, refine it with them, and launch it together.
