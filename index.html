How I work

A simple process.

More on the process
  1. 1

    Discovery

    A quick call or written brief to understand the problem, the audience, and what success looks like.

  2. 2

    Proposal

    A clear scope, timeline, and price, agreed on before any code gets written.

  3. 3

    Build

    Custom-coded, with regular check-ins so there are no surprises at handoff.

  4. 4

    Launch

    Deployed, tested, and handed off with everything documented.

  5. 5

    Support

    Ongoing fixes and features from someone who already knows the codebase.
