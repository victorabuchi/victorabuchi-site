Service 02

Custom Web Apps / SaaS Development

Full-stack platforms built to scale: multi-tenant architecture, sign-in, payments and everything in between. I have designed and shipped several from scratch, so I know where they get hard.

What’s included

Everything it takes.

Multi-tenant from day one

One codebase serving many separate, branded customers, each with its own isolated data.

Accounts, roles and permissions

Sign-in with JWT and Google OAuth, and role-based access so every user sees exactly what they should.

Payments and billing

Subscription plans and checkout with Stripe.

APIs and integrations

Fastify REST APIs, and connections to services like Shopify, market-data feeds and email.

Data you can trust

PostgreSQL databases, typed with TypeScript, with business logic kept in one place on the backend.

Shipped and running

Deployed on Render with auto-deploy from GitHub, on your own domain.
