Website Redesign / Migration

Moving a business off an aging platform (WordPress, page builders) onto something faster, cleaner and easier to maintain, without losing what already works.

What’s included

Everything it takes.

Audit first

I list what already works, from pages and content to links and the parts people rely on, so nothing valuable is lost in the move.

A cleaner foundation

Rebuilt in modern code such as Next.js, instead of layers of plugins and page-builder markup.

Search visibility protected

A proper SEO pass, a sitemap, structured data and language alternates on the new site.

Better on phones

Mobile-first layouts, including sensible footers and navigation menus on small screens.

A clean handover

Everything documented, so the next change is easy.
