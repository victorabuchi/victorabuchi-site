Process

When you bring the brief

For client work: we agree the scope first, then I build to it.

  • 01 Discovery A quick call or written brief to understand the problem, the audience, and what success looks like.
  • 02 Proposal A clear scope, timeline, and price, agreed on before any code gets written.
  • 03 Build Custom-coded, with regular check-ins so there are no surprises at handoff.
  • 04 Launch Deployed, tested, and handed off with everything documented.
  • 05 Support Ongoing fixes and features from someone who already knows the codebase.

When I spot the problem

For problems I notice first: I do the work up front, then show it to the people it’s for. That’s how Rannikon and Kuopas began.

  • 01 Spot the problem Something slow, manual, or error-prone that people live with every day.
  • 02 Identify what’s broken Work out the real cause, and who it affects, before writing any code.
  • 03 Solve it Build the complete solution on my own, from scratch, until it works.
  • 04 Present it Show it to the company or organization involved, refine it with them, and launch it together.
